# Hypha app launch: onboarding and offer

## The company

Hypha (growhypha.com) is a small UK brand that teaches people to grow mushrooms at home. It already sells two grow kits on its Shopify store: the Standard Mushroom Grow Kit (£34.99) and the Big Batch Mushroom Grow Kit (£47.99). The kits ship pre-sterilised: "The hard part is already done. Sterilising grain and substrate is where most first grows die. We've done it for you." The site offers multi-buy discounts (buy 2 save 10%, buy 3+ save 20%) and says each kit includes one free month of the app.

Hypha's social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) have about 150,000 followers. About 1,400 people have grown their first flush with Hypha's kits and courses. Most of the audience is aged 18 to 34 and found Hypha through short-form video.

Hypha's Trustpilot page scores 3.9 ("Great") from 7 reviews. The positive reviews include "Grew almost a kilo of mushrooms in a month and a half", "Absolutely class product", "Perfect kit. I am a beginner and it went well", "Can't fault the service at all. With you from start to finish", "Lots of good advice and direction on the community app" and "Great service and advice whenever you need it". A recent 1-star review is titled "Bunch of crooks".

The main free alternatives are YouTube tutorials and Reddit growing communities. The founder describes those communities as "90% gatekeeping and 10% useful advice", and says one of the biggest was recently taken down.

## The product being launched

The Hypha app is a mobile app built on the Kinflo no-code platform. It offers:

- step-by-step courses for every stage of a grow
- a community feed where growers post and comment
- 1-to-1 access to mushroom-growing experts
- a DIY cultivation course for growing without a kit
- a free grow kit delivered by the end of the week (on the annual plan)
- a guarantee: "Grow doesn't work out? We make it work, or your money back."

The brand voice is blunt and cheeky. The app makes no medicinal claims and does not name species in onboarding ("grow any mushroom"). First harvest is promised in 6 to 8 weeks.

## Pricing

There is a hard paywall at the end of onboarding: no free tier, and you must pick a plan before creating an account.

| Plan | Price | Includes |

| All in (annual, featured) | £89.99/year ("£7.50/month, billed yearly") | All courses, full community, free grow kit, 1-to-1 expert access, money-back guarantee |
| Starter (monthly) | £12.99/month | Courses and community |
| All in, Standard kit owners | £54.99 first year, then £89.99/year | Kit price taken off the first year (needs order number) |
| All in, Big Batch kit owners | £41.99 first year, then £89.99/year | Kit price taken off the first year (needs order number) |

The annual card carries a "SAVE 42%" badge (12 × £12.99 = £155.88). A 7-day free trial on the annual plan is planned but not shown on the paywall. The paywall footer says "Cancel anytime · Restore purchase". The button says "Start growing".

## The onboarding flow

Every question and text screen shows a cartoon mascot: a curly-haired man in a grey hoodie, holding growing equipment or mushrooms.

### Opening (everyone)

1. "Most people's first grow fails. Yours doesn't have to." Options: I'm new here / I already have an account.
2. "150,000 people follow us." "1,400 of them have grown their first flush with our kits and courses."
3. "Got a Hypha grow kit?" Options: Yes, the Standard kit / Yes, the Big Batch kit / No.
4. If no kit: "Which one's you?" Options: Total beginner, never grown a thing / Done a kit, now I want more / I already grow, I want better results / I want to grow enough to sell.

There are five routes: kit owners, beginners, kit graduates, DIY growers and side-hustlers. Each ends at its own paywall.

### Kit owners (came from the QR code in the kit box)

They are asked where their kit is at, whether they've grown anything before, where it will live, what they're most worried about (doing it wrong, mould, nothing happening, wasting the kit), what's next after this kit, and when they're starting.

Key messages:
- "We do the hard part. You follow simple instructions, and pick up a skill you'll have for life."
- "Everything you need, in one place."
- "You won't be guessing. Stuck at any point? Ask our experts 1-to-1. And if it doesn't work out, we make it work or give you your money back."
- "One grow. A lifetime of mushrooms. Enough for you, your friends and your family. Buying that much would cost a fortune."
- "Your kit pays for itself. We'll take what you paid for it off your first year. We just need your order number."

Paywall: "Your kit-owner price. We've taken what you paid for your kit off your first year." It shows £54.99 or £41.99 for the first year, then £89.99/year.

### Beginners (the main route)

They are asked:
- whether they've grown anything before
- what got them into it: cooking, saw it on social media, saving money, need a new hobby, might sell them one day
- where they would grow
- what's stopping them, with a tailored reply to each answer:
  - "No idea what to buy" → "That's the easy bit."
  - "Every video says something different" → "Because half of them are guessing."
  - "Scared I'll waste my money" → "Fair. Most first grows do fail. So here's the deal…" (the guarantee)
  - "Worried it'll go mouldy" → "Mould is beatable."
  - "It looks too sciencey" → "You don't need a lab coat."
- a quiz, "You spot white fluff on your grow. What do you do?", answered with "That's probably mycelium. It's meant to be there."
- how much time they have each week, answered with "Don't worry. It only takes an hour to set up, then some waiting, then an hour to harvest."
- "Honestly, could you get a harvest on your own right now?"
- when they're starting

The route closes with "Your first harvest, 8 weeks from now."

Paywall: "Your first harvest, sorted." Checklist: exactly what to buy, what to do each week, help when something looks off.

### Kit graduates

They are asked how their kit went (smashed it / one flush then it died / it never fruited / it went mouldy), how many grows they've done, what they want next (cut costs, scale up, liquid culture, new types, a business), whether they know why it worked or didn't, and whether they could grow from scratch tomorrow.

Key messages:
- "That kit was level one."
- "The mushroom industry grows about 9% a year. It's already worth over $70 billion worldwide."
- "Thinking about a mushroom business? Send us a DM and we'll advise you."
- "Off kits in 8 weeks."

Paywall: "From kit to growing on your own."

### DIY growers

They are asked what keeps going wrong (contamination, stalls and won't pin, tiny flushes, works once then doesn't), their setup (monotub, all-in-one bags, shotgun fruiting chamber, grow tent), how many grows they've binned to contamination, and whether they track temperature and humidity.

Key messages:
- "That's not bad luck."
- "Every binned tub is weeks gone."
- "Guessing is where pins die."
- "Consistent harvests in 8 weeks."

Paywall: "Fewer binned tubs."

### Side-hustlers

They are asked about growing experience, what they want it to make (pocket money / £100 to £500 a month / enough to quit my job), how much space they have, what worries them about selling (not growing enough, losing batches, finding buyers, the legal side), hours per week, and when they want their first sale.

Key messages:
- "Start with one tub, not ten."
- "Contam is what kills profit."
- "Sell-ready in 8 weeks."

Paywall: "Get to your first sale."

### After the paywall

Sign-up: "Last step. Make an account so your plan's saved." Then email, username ("What other growers will see") and password.

## Launch plan

The app launches to Hypha's existing social following and kit buyers. It will be promoted through short-form video on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, and through a QR code inside every grow kit box. The aim of the onboarding is to sell the annual "All in" plan.

## Known open questions

- Payments (RevenueCat, App Store and Play intro offers) are not fully wired up. The 7-day trial and kit-owner first-year pricing depend on them.
- The order-number field can be left blank and still unlock the kit-owner discount.
- It is unclear whether Starter members get 1-to-1 expert access, which is listed under "Only with All In".
- Apple may read a mushroom-growing app as psilocybin-related. The mascot was changed to hold lion's mane, reishi and oyster mushrooms.
- Physical goods (the free kit) can't be sold inside an Apple in-app subscription, so the kit is arranged by email after purchase.
- It is undecided whether the free app month bundled with kits stacks with the kit-owner discount.
