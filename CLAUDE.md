# vids

Hypha Origins: an Instagram reel series about mushroom species and strains
around the world, built on the Origin Map (`origin-map/`) plus cartoon b-roll
generated in OpenArt.

- Scripts follow `docs/script-playbook.md` (Johnny Harris method: hook that
  points at a thing, reveal before 25s, a named person, a place, a receipt,
  a kicker; 75 to 95s). Read it before writing or editing any script.
- Episode assets and shot lists live in `origin-map/shots/<episode>/`.
- The map's series intro shot is `origin-map/shots/intro-shot.js`; render
  with `origin-map/scripts/shoot-intro.mjs` (see `origin-map/README.md`).
- Voice: first person, lowercase in captions, no hype.
