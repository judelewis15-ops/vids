Mate if you wanna grow mushrooms
Stop dicking around with heat mats
I live in Glasgow so I know what it's like to have slow grows due to the cold
That doesn't mean that putting a heat source directly on your bag is gonna do anything but dry out your grains and substrate
Mushrooms are 90% water we don't want that
Instead what you can do is plug a heater bar in a few feet away from your grow
And plug that into an inkbird temperature controller
The trick is to heat the air and room around your grow, not the grow itself
