## Product references used for shots 6 and 7

Heater bar = UK tubular ("tube") heater, the kind sold as an "Eco Magic Mushroom Tube Heater":
one long slim white enamelled steel tube, roughly 50 mm diameter and 2 to 3 ft long, 60 W per
foot, sitting horizontally on two small metal bracket feet, cable and UK plug out of one end,
small on/off indicator light near that end.

Inkbird ITC-308: black plastic box about 140 x 68 x 33 mm with rounded corners. Front face has
a dark display window with bright digits (current temperature and set temperature shown
together) and three small round buttons beneath it. Two cables leave the bottom edge: the power
lead and a long thin temperature-probe wire ending in a small metal sensor tip. Two sockets on
the back, one for heating and one for cooling, which is where the heater plugs in. Brand text
was deliberately left off the artwork.
